This information may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, depending on your location and consent settings.

I do not control the data collected by Google AdMob. For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

I do not collect or store personal data directly through my apps.
