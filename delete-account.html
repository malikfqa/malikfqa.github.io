Pak Cricket Scorer

Delete your online account and cloud data

How to delete (in the app)

  1. Open Pak Cricket Scorer and go to Profile.
  2. Sign in with the Google account you used for online features.
  3. Tap Delete account & online data.
  4. Read both confirmation screens carefully, then confirm.
Deletion is permanent. Online account, published team, player profile, photos, and other online data are removed. Nothing is kept for backup or recovery.

What is deleted

  • Your Pak Cricket Scorer online sign-in (Supabase account tied to Google)
  • Your published online team, roster, logos, and team cloud stats/history for that team
  • Your online player profile, photo, and career cloud stats
  • Pending confirmation requests you submitted

What is not deleted

  • Matches and scores stored only on your device (local data stays until you uninstall or clear app data)
  • Your Google account (we do not delete Google)
  • Other teams’ win/loss or history lines about matches involving you (those remain on their records)
