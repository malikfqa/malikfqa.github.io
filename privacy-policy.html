Privacy Policy

Developer: Fahad Qayyum Awan  •  Contact: malik.fahad1990@gmail.com

Effective date: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use my Android applications and related software services (“Services”), including Pak Cricket Scorer.

Summary

  • Most app data (matches, scores, statistics) is stored locally on your device.
  • Sign-in and online teams are optional. You can use core scoring features without an account.
  • If you sign in and publish a team online, limited data is stored on secure cloud servers.
  • Some apps may show ads through Google AdMob.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Unless you choose optional online features, Pak Cricket Scorer stores data on your device, such as:

  • Team names, player names, and captain names
  • Match details, scores, statistics, and match history
  • Generated match report PDFs (in the app’s private storage)
  • Backup files you create (stored on your device unless you share them)

This local data is not uploaded to my servers unless you explicitly use online publish or backup/share features.

Optional Account Sign-In (Google)

Some features (such as publishing a team online) may require you to sign in with Google. This is optional.

If you sign in, I receive and process through third-party authentication services:

  • Your email address (from your Google account)
  • A user account identifier used to link your account to online features

I use this information only to provide account-related and online team features. I do not sell your personal information.

Optional Online Teams (Cloud)

If you choose to publish a team online in Pak Cricket Scorer, the following may be stored on cloud servers operated by my service providers:

  • Public team ID you choose (so others can find your team)
  • Team name
  • Player names in that published squad
  • Account identifier linking the published team to your sign-in
  • Last activity date for that online team

Match scores and full match history are not uploaded when you publish a team online. Other users can import your published squad by public team ID; they do not receive your private match data.

Limits that apply to online teams:

  • One Google account may publish one active online team at a time.
  • Online teams with no update for 90 days may be automatically deleted from our servers.
  • You may remove your team from online at any time in the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some apps may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • App interaction data

This information may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, depending on your location and consent settings.

I do not control the data collected by Google AdMob. For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Third-Party Services

Apps may use third-party services that process data under their own privacy policies, including:

  • Google — sign-in (Google account)
  • Supabase — cloud storage for optional online team features
  • Google AdMob — advertising

I do not control all data practices of these providers. Please review their policies for more information.

Data Deletion

You can:

  • Remove your team from online in the app (deletes that team’s data from our servers)
  • Sign out of your online account in the app
  • Uninstall the app to remove local data from your device (subject to your device/OS behavior)
  • Contact me to request deletion of account-related or online data

Email: malik.fahad1990@gmail.com

For data collected by Google AdMob or Google Sign-In, please also use Google’s account and privacy controls where applicable.

Data Security

Online data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). I use reasonable technical and organizational measures through my service providers, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

International Users

My apps are available globally. By using optional online features, you acknowledge that information may be processed and stored outside your country of residence through my service providers.

Children’s Privacy

These Services are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact me so that appropriate steps can be taken.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area or California), you may have rights under applicable privacy laws, including access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing.

To exercise rights related to online account or published team data, contact me at the email above. For AdMob or Google account data, refer to Google’s privacy tools and policies.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
